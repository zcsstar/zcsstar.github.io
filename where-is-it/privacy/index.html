隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 3 日

「放哪儿」（英文名 Where Is It?）是个人开发者 Chi Zhang 开发的物品收纳记录 App：拍下东西和它放的地方，圈出位置，以后很快找到。本页说明这个 App 会处理哪些数据。

简要说明

  • 不需要注册账号。开发者没有自己的服务器，不收集你的个人信息，App 里没有统计分析工具。
  • 你记录的一切——物品名称、关键词、分类、备注、存放位置、照片和上面的标记、取走放回的历史——都只加密保存在这台设备上，不会上传。
  • 备份只在你自己导出时生成，用你设的密码加密，发到哪里由你决定。
  • 免费版通过 Google AdMob 展示少量广告；Pro 版没有广告，也不会启动广告组件。

保存在你设备上的数据

  • 记录保存在加密数据库里（SQLCipher，AES-256），照片单独加密（AES-256-GCM）。密钥在这台设备上生成，存放在系统的钥匙串（iOS）或密钥库（Android）里。
  • 拍照或从相册选的照片会缩小、去掉拍摄地点等信息后加密保存；不会存进系统相册。App 只读取你选中的照片。
  • 可以开启 App 锁，用面容 ID、触控 ID、指纹或手机密码解锁。验证由系统完成，App 拿不到你的生物识别数据。
  • 开发者看不到这些数据，也不会把它们用于广告。删除的物品在回收站保留 30 天后永久删除；删除 App 会清除全部数据。

备份与系统备份

  • 「备份与恢复」导出的文件用你设的密码加密（Argon2id 和 AES-256-GCM），没有密码谁也打不开，开发者也不能。文件通过系统的分享面板保存或发送到你选的地方（比如「文件」、iCloud 云盘、Google 云端硬盘或聊天软件），由那些服务按它们的政策处理。
  • iPhone：App 的加密数据会包含在你的 iCloud 或电脑备份里，换新 iPhone 时随系统迁移。Android：App 的数据不参与 Google 的云端备份和设备间迁移，换手机请用导出的备份文件。

分享

你分享一件物品时，App 生成一张图片交给系统的分享面板，发到哪里、给谁由你决定。

语音输入

备注的语音输入用系统自带的语音识别。手机支持时只在本机识别；只有你在「设置 › 语音输入」里允许联网后，声音才会交给 Apple 或 Google 的识别服务处理，适用它们各自的隐私政策。App 不保存录音。

广告（Google AdMob，仅免费版）

按照 Google 的说明，AdMob 的软件包会自动收集：

  • IP 地址，可能用来估计设备的大致位置；
  • 设备标识符：Android 上是广告 ID 和 app set ID；iOS 上 App 不请求跟踪权限，所以 AdMob 拿不到广告标识符（IDFA），但可能使用只在本 App 或本开发者范围内有效的设备标识符；
  • 广告和使用信息，比如看过的广告、点击和 App 启动；
  • 诊断信息，比如 App 的启动时间、卡顿和耗电情况，以及崩溃日志。

这些数据用于投放和统计广告、控制同一广告出现的次数以及防止作弊，由 Google 处理，适用 Google 隐私权政策和Google 如何使用来自使用其服务的网站或应用的信息。开发者拿不到这些数据。你记录的物品、照片、位置和备注不会提供给 AdMob，也不会用于投放广告。

你的选择

  • 欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士：App 会显示 Google 的同意表单，你可以同意、拒绝或逐项选择，之后可以在「设置 › 隐私与广告 › 隐私选项」里随时更改。
  • 美国设有相关隐私法的州：可以在「设置 › 隐私与广告 › 隐私选项」里选择不出售或分享你的个人信息。
  • Android：可以在系统设置的「广告」选项里删除或重置广告 ID。
  • iOS：App 不请求跟踪权限，不会进行跨 App 跟踪。
  • 升级 Pro 后不再展示广告，也不再启动广告组件。

购买 Pro

Pro 通过 App Store 或 Google Play 购买，付款由 Apple 或 Google 处理，开发者拿不到你的付款信息。App 只向商店询问这个账号是否买过 Pro。

App 更新

App 的程序更新通过 Expo 的更新服务（EAS Update）下载。请求里有 App 版本、系统平台，以及更新组件在设备上生成的随机安装 ID，用于发放和统计更新，适用 Expo 隐私政策。

权限

  • 相机：拍物品和存放位置的照片。
  • 照片：从相册选照片（只读取你选中的那几张）。
  • 麦克风和语音识别：语音输入备注。
  • 面容 ID / 生物识别：App 锁。
  • 网络：广告、App 更新和购买。

儿童

App 适合所有年龄使用，但不是专门面向儿童的服务，开发者不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

政策变更

如果 App 增加了会改变数据处理方式的功能（比如同步或账号），本页会更新，并修改上面的日期。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

放哪儿 (Where Is It?) is an app by the individual developer Chi Zhang for recording where things are put away: a photo of the item and of its spot, the place circled, found again in seconds. This page explains what data the app handles.

Summary

  • No account. The developer runs no servers of their own and collects no personal information, and the app has no analytics.
  • Everything you record — names, keywords, categories, notes, places, photos and the marks on them, the history of taking things out and putting them back — stays on this device, encrypted, and is never uploaded.
  • A backup is made only when you export one, encrypted with your password, sent where you choose.
  • The free version shows a few ads through Google AdMob; Pro has none and never starts the ad SDK.

Data stored on your device

  • Records are kept in an encrypted database (SQLCipher, AES-256) and photos are encrypted one by one (AES-256-GCM). The keys are made on this device and kept in the system's keychain (iOS) or keystore (Android).
  • Photos from the camera or your library are shrunk, stripped of location and other details, and encrypted; they are never saved to your photo library. The app reads only the photos you pick.
  • An optional app lock opens with Face ID, Touch ID, a fingerprint or the device passcode. The system does the checking; the app never receives biometric data.
  • The developer can't see this data and never uses it for advertising. Deleted items stay in the trash for 30 days, then are deleted for good; deleting the app removes all of it.

Backups and system backups

  • A file exported from Backup & restore is encrypted with your password (Argon2id and AES-256-GCM); without it nobody can open the file, the developer included. It goes through the system's share sheet to where you choose (Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, a chat app…), which handle it under their own policies.
  • iPhone: the app's encrypted data is part of your iCloud or computer backups and moves to a new iPhone with them. Android: the app's data is left out of Google's cloud backups and device-to-device transfers; use an exported backup file to move phones.

Sharing

When you share an item, the app makes a picture and hands it to the system's share sheet; where it goes and to whom is up to you.

Voice input

Dictating a note uses the system's own speech recognition, on the device where it can. Only if you allow online recognition in Settings › Voice input does the audio go to Apple's or Google's recognition service, under their privacy policies. The app keeps no recordings.

Advertising (Google AdMob, free version only)

According to Google, the AdMob SDK automatically collects:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate the device's general location;
  • device identifiers: on Android, the advertising ID and the app set ID; on iOS the app doesn't ask for tracking permission, so AdMob gets no advertising identifier (IDFA), but it may use identifiers limited to this app or developer;
  • ad and usage information, such as the ads you see, taps and app launches;
  • diagnostics, such as launch time, hangs and energy use, and crash logs.

Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, limit how often the same ad appears and prevent fraud, under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. The developer has no access to it. Your items, photos, places and notes are never passed to AdMob or used to target ads.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the app shows Google's consent form, where you can consent, refuse or choose per purpose, and you can change your choice any time in Settings › Privacy and ads › Privacy options.
  • US states with privacy laws: you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information in Settings › Privacy and ads › Privacy options.
  • Android: you can delete or reset the advertising ID in the system's Ads settings.
  • iOS: the app doesn't ask for tracking permission and doesn't track you across apps.
  • With Pro there are no ads and the ad SDK is never started.

Buying Pro

Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play; Apple or Google handle the payment and the developer never sees your payment details. The app only asks the store whether the account owns Pro.

App updates

App updates are downloaded from Expo's update service (EAS Update). The request carries the app version, the platform and a random installation ID that the update component creates on the device, used to deliver and count updates, under the Expo Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Camera: photos of your things and the spots where you put them.
  • Photos: picking photos from your library (only the ones you pick are read).
  • Microphone and speech recognition: dictating notes.
  • Face ID / biometrics: the app lock.
  • Network: ads, app updates and purchases.

Children

The app suits all ages but isn't directed at children, and the developer doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If the app gains a feature that changes how data is handled, such as sync or accounts, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
