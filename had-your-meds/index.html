给全家的药，一个清楚的记录

  • 拍照预填在手机上读出标签上的字和条码，填好药名、规格、用法和有效期。每一项都由你确认，原始照片随时能看。
  • 中英对照，标明参考常见的用法句子和 1,800 多个药名有整理好的中英对照；其余句子可以用手机上的机器翻译。译文都标为参考。
  • 全家的药箱按家人和分类整理，过期、临期、快用完的药都会标出来，方便你留意。
  • 按时提醒，一点就记到时间手机会提醒，点「已服」就记下；补记、跳过都可以，每个人的服药记录清清楚楚。
  • 长辈模式装在长辈自己的手机上：字大，只显示现在该吃的药，一点就记录，还能读出来听。
  • 只在你的手机上，加密保存不用注册，没有服务器，开发者看不到你的内容。照片不进系统相册，备份用你自己的密码加密。

吃药没？帮你记下家里的药，按你设的时间提醒，并提供标签的翻译参考。它不提供医疗建议，不计算剂量，也不判断药能不能一起吃；它不是医疗器械，不诊断、治疗或预防任何疾病。怎么用药，以药房标签和医生、药剂师的说明为准，有疑问请问药剂师或医生。

Had Your Meds?

Your family's medicines, recorded with a photo.

Photograph a medicine box and its label. The app reads the name, strength, directions and expiry on your phone, with reference translations between Chinese and English. Check each item, save it to your family's cabinet and set reminders — then see at a glance who takes what, and whether they have.

A clear record of your family's medicines

  • Filled in from a photoThe text and barcode on the label are read on your phone to fill in the name, strength, directions and expiry. You check every item, and the original photo is always there to look at.
  • Chinese and English, marked as referenceCommon directions and over 1,800 medicine names come with prepared Chinese–English references; other sentences can use your phone's machine translation. Every translation is marked as a reference.
  • The family cabinetSorted by person and category, with expired, soon-to-expire and running-low medicines marked so you can keep an eye on them.
  • Reminders, recorded with a tapYour phone reminds you on time; tap Taken to record it, or record it later or skip it. Each person's history stays clear.
  • Elder modeOn an older family member's own phone: large text, only the medicines due now, one tap to record, and it can read them aloud.
  • Only on your phone, encryptedNo account and no server: the developer never sees your records. Photos stay out of your photo library, and backups are sealed with your own password.
Please note

Had Your Meds? keeps a record of your family's medicines, reminds you at the times you set and gives translation references for labels. It doesn't give medical advice, calculate doses or check whether medicines can be taken together. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any disease. Follow the pharmacy label and your doctor or pharmacist, and ask them if you are unsure.

Free for up to 3 family members (you included), with unlimited medicines and reminders. Pro, one purchase: no limit on family members, no ads.
